Doorstep is a free, open-source app that shares files between your phone and your computer over your own Wi-Fi — with no internet, no cloud and no accounts.

Pair once, and you are done: Doorstep keeps your devices connected automatically. Drop a file into a drop zone on your computer and it arrives on your phone — even with the Doorstep app closed. Share from your phone and it lands on your computer just as easily.

Highlights:
- Pair once — devices reconnect on their own, no QR codes to re-scan
- Receive files with the app closed, via a quiet background service you control
- Drop zones — folders on your computer that send themselves to your phone
- Live folder browser — browse and pull files from your computer on your phone
- Trust levels — mark a device as personal (permanent) or temporary (this session only)
- Encrypted transfers over your local network. Nothing ever leaves your network.

Doorstep works even when the internet is down. No ads, no tracking, no sign-ups.
